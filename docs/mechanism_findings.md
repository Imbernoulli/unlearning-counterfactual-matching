# Calibration-only mechanism findings

These probes use training images and the fixed 1,250-image selection set.
They never load official test, audit, or Retrain predictions. Original and
candidate feature/gradient comparisons use the same fixed examples. The
first-round paired test results remain frozen separately.

## What the stagewise probe actually shows

For schedule A (full forget pass followed by full retain pass), seed 42:

| Epoch | KS after forget | KS after retain | Forget-minus-selection error after forget (pp) | After retain (pp) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 0.0976 | 0.1140 | -6.809 | -5.582 |
| 2 | 0.1085 | 0.1159 | -4.382 | -4.707 |
| 3 | 0.1142 | 0.1205 | -3.938 | -4.204 |
| 10 | 0.1397 | 0.1339 | -2.680 | -2.658 |

A retain pass can reverse a confidence-matching improvement, especially early.
The effect is not universal: epoch 10 improves KS after retention. Classification
error and confidence matching can move in different directions. This is evidence
against a single explanation that every retain update simply erases forgetting.
A/B/C have equal sample passes, but A has 177 optimizer steps per epoch and B/C
159, so they are not pure order-only or equal-step experiments.

## Feature/head interventions

For joint updates without the Original anchor, frozen BN, epoch 2, fixed 1,024
examples per group:

| Hybrid | Forget error (%) | Selection error (%) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Original features + Original head | 0.000 | 6.641 |
| Original features + candidate head | 0.000 | 6.641 |
| Candidate features + Original head | 2.637 | 7.129 |
| Candidate features + candidate head | 2.637 | 7.129 |

Most changed predictions in this checkpoint follow the representation. This
motivates training only the last block or head as separate controlled candidates;
it does not prove that training a different head alone could never work. Forget
linear CKA is 0.972, selection CKA 0.979, retain-subset CKA 0.984. BN buffers are
exactly unchanged in the frozen-BN run.

## Gradient directions

For fixed clean batches of 256 forget and 256 retain images, unweighted
parameter gradients of log-odds MSE and retain CE have these cosines:

| Checkpoint | Whole model | Layer 4 | Head |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Original | -0.285 | -0.692 | -0.464 |
| Sequential A, epoch 10 | -0.634 | -0.869 | -0.754 |
| Joint C + frozen BN, epoch 2 | 0.181 | 0.303 | 0.401 |

The gradients conflict at some checkpoints and align at others. Measurements
are local, use clean images, omit optimizer coefficients/momentum, and do not
measure every augmented training batch. A PCGrad comparison tests whether
handling such conflicts helps; these observations do not establish causation.

## Target fit and a remaining confidence discrepancy

For schedule A epoch 10, the lowest Original-confidence decile has mean
log-odds residual +0.741 relative to its fixed targets; the next decile +1.003.
All ten deciles have positive average residual. The fixed-batch full forget
log-odds RMSE is still 0.910 (Original 2.424). This suggests incomplete fitting,
with the low-confidence target tail particularly important for classification.

In the C/frozen run, the full-set confidence KS versus selection is 0.1400
at initialization, 0.0957 at epoch 2, and 0.1348 at epoch 10. Its maximizing
confidence threshold moves from approximately 0.9961 to 0.9967 to 0.9991.
Consequently the predeclared full-CDF selection rule measures discrepancies
beyond classification error and beyond any one attack threshold. It should
not be equated to the reported SVC MIA gap. The selection rule remains fixed;
these diagnostics do not retrospectively choose a more favorable test metric.
